## First Steps Walkthrough: Surviving Your First In-Game Week

Maximizing your limited time in the **Moonfrost demo** requires efficient planning from the moment you take control of your character. Careless stamina usage on day one can leave you exhausted before noon, slowing down your progress and limiting your exploration.

### Day 1: Clearing a Perimeter and Planting Starters

Resist the urge to clear your entire plot immediately. Heavy boulders and thick tree stumps will rapidly drain your starting energy bar. Instead, carve out a compact 3x3 or 4x4 perimeter directly in front of your cabin doorstep.

*   Equip your scythe to sweep away tall weeds without consuming valuable stamina.
*   Switch to your basic hoe to till your designated planting tiles.
*   Deposit your starter seeds into the moist earth, then water each tile thoroughly.
*   Head to the nearest water source to refill your watering can before dusk arrives.

### Day 2 to Day 4: Foraging Routes and Crafting Your First Chest

Inventory management becomes a real challenge as soon as you begin picking up wild flora and stone chips. Spend your mornings tending to your freshly planted seeds, then spend early afternoons charting the wilderness pathways encircling your plot.

Collecting wild berries serves two vital functions: it reserves hard-earned coins by providing free stamina snacks, and it yields items you can turn into craftable supplies. Once you gather enough loose branches, construct your first wooden storage chest. Keeping your active inventory clear prevents the frustration of leaving rare wild finds behind in the woods.

## Essential Tips and Strategies for Demo Explorers

To make every second count inside the **Moonfrost demo**, keep these tactical guidelines in mind during your playthrough:

*   **Honor the Stamina Threshold:** Never push your energy meter down to absolute zero. Collapsing from overexertion forces an emergency wake-up penalty the next morning, halving your available daily stamina pool.
*   **Monitor In-Game Clocks:** The day-night cycle moves steadily. Set aside the hour between 10:00 PM and midnight to organize your inventory, review your quest log, and rest your avatar.
*   **Save Rain for Deep Wilderness Forays:** Rainy days are an absolute blessing because mother nature waters all your tilled crop fields automatically. Use that saved energy to forage deeper into untamed woods or focus on gathering timber.
*   **Submit Structured Feedback:** Remember that playtest builds exist primarily to refine gameplay balance. If you encounter clipping issues, audio stutters, or tool balance quirks, submit detailed bug reports via the official feedback form in the pause menu.

### Does progress from the Moonfrost demo carry over to the final release?
In most cases, save files generated in early alpha versions or event-specific demos will not carry over to the final release build. Developers often rebalance economy systems, revise maps, and modify save formats between early testing and commercial launch. Treat your demo time as an enjoyable sandbox to learn mechanics without worrying about permanent progress.

### Can I play the Moonfrost demo with friends in multiplayer?
Multiplayer availability depends entirely on the active testing phase. While the completed title is explicitly designed around collaborative and social life-sim gameplay, certain focused demo builds prioritize single-player stability and basic mechanics testing. Check the patch notes accompanying your specific build download to confirm whether online co-op is enabled.
