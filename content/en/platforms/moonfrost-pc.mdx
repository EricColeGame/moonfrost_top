## Optimizing Graphics and Display Settings on PC

Fine-tuning your **Moonfrost PC** display options will yield the best visual clarity without taxing your hardware unnecessarily. Because the title showcases intricate pixel art, improper display scaling can cause blurriness or pixel distortion.

Follow these simple optimization practices to maintain crisp sprites:

- **Display Mode:** Choose **Exclusive Fullscreen** or **Borderless Windowed** matching your display's native resolution.
- **Pixel Perfect Scaling:** Enable this setting inside the graphics submenu. It locks sprite rendering to integer multipliers, eliminating uneven pixel borders.
- **V-Sync & Framerate Cap:** Lock your framerate to your monitor’s refresh rate (60 Hz, 120 Hz, or 144 Hz) to prevent screen tearing while moving across large fields.
- **Dynamic Weather Effects:** If you experience dips during stormy days, reduce particle density by one level to maintain steady performance.
- **Interface Scale:** Increase UI scaling to 125% or 150% if playing on high-DPI displays (1440p or 4K) for comfortable text readability.
