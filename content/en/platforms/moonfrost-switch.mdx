### Optimizing Handheld PCs for Cozy Sessions

If you opt to use a portable PC as your substitute for a **Moonfrost Switch** setup, a few quick tweaks can dramatically extend your battery life. Community reports indicate that capping your device's refresh rate to 40Hz or 45Hz provides a smooth visual experience for pixel-art movement while cutting power consumption nearly in half. Lowering your device's thermal design power (TDP) to around 10 watts is usually more than enough to handle top-down farming mechanics without triggering loud cooling fans.

Additionally, if you are playing on a device without native controller bindings enabled in an early build, you can utilize community controller layouts through Steam Input. Mapping the left trackpad to act as a radial menu for farming tools—such as your axe, pickaxe, and watering can—makes switching equipment faster than using a standard scroll wheel.
