## Frequently Asked Questions About Moonfrost Social Quests

### Do Moonfrost social quests expire if I do not finish them immediately?
Standard bulletin board errands typically come with a timer between 24 and 72 in-game hours. If you fail to turn in the requested items within that timeframe, the errand will simply disappear without a friendship penalty. However, unique character vignette quests and large community projects remain active indefinitely until completed.

### Can I complete Moonfrost social quests entirely in single-player mode?
Yes. While cooperative projects are balanced to encourage multiplayer contributions, every project scale adjusts so that solo players can steadily fulfill requirements on their own. Playing cooperatively simply speeds up the material gathering phase for massive town expansions.

### What happens if I give a villager the wrong item during a quest?
Handing an NPC an incorrect item treats the exchange as a standard daily gift rather than a quest fulfillment. If the item happens to be something they dislike or hate, your friendship score could temporarily decrease. Always double-check your inventory and ensure the quest icon appears in the interaction prompt before confirming the delivery.

### How do social quests impact romance options in Moonfrost?
Completing personal favor quests is required to unlock deeper relationship tiers with eligible romance candidates. Passing major heart milestones usually triggers a custom narrative quest that must be finished before you can present commitment tokens or unlock romantic housing options.
