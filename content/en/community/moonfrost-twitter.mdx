### Preparation Steps for Testing Waves

1. **Enable Mobile Push Alerts:** Turn on post notifications for official handles so you receive real-time pings when sign-up links go live.
2. **Prepare Your System Specs:** Keep your PC hardware details (GPU, CPU, RAM, OS version) readily available for quick entry on intake forms.
3. **Verify Survey Authenticity:** Ensure any application link originates directly from official handles rather than third-party mirrors.
4. **Join the Companion Discord:** Many playtest invitations distributed via social posts require linked Discord accounts for role authentication and private feedback reporting.

Following these practices ensures you can submit your details cleanly without running into closed quotas or expired links.

## Best Practices to Filter News and Avoid Misinformation

The excitement surrounding anticipated titles can unfortunately attract spam accounts, deceptive links, and unverified rumors. Protecting your digital security while browsing social game feeds is just as critical as staying informed.

Because social platforms undergo frequent changes regarding verification marks, relying purely on badges is no longer foolproof. Players must carefully verify handle names and outgoing domain destinations before clicking external links.

| Verification Check | Legitimate Indicator | Red Flag / Warning Sign |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Account Handle** | Matches exact spelling linked from the official website | Extra underscores, swapped letters, or appended numbers |
| **Outgoing URLs** | Direct links to official domains or verified Steam pages | Shortened mystery links (`bit.ly`, suspicious redirect domains) |
| **Playtest Requirements** | Free sign-up via standard Google Forms or web portals | Requests for wallet phrases, upfront payments, or file downloads outside Steam |
| **Post Tone & Language** | Professional, enthusiastic, focused on development | High-pressure urgency ("Claim in 5 minutes!"), vague giveaway promises |

Whenever you see news regarding release dates or sudden monetization structures, cross-reference the claim with other channels. If a major announcement does not appear across the studio's verified Discord or website alongside their feed, treat it cautiously until confirmed.
