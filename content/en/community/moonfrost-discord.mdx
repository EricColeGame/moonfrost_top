## Server Etiquette, Safety, and Community Guidelines

A cozy simulation title naturally attracts a warm, welcoming player base. To preserve this friendly atmosphere, server moderators enforce strict behavior standards. Respectful communication, inclusive dialogue, and zero tolerance for harassment are pillars of the community culture.

Because high-profile indie games attract bad actors, security awareness is paramount. Scammers frequently masquerade as community managers, sending direct messages that promise instant alpha access or exclusive in-game items in exchange for clicking third-party links or connecting digital wallets.

Experienced members of the Moonfrost Discord often recommend turning off direct messages from server members if you prefer an uninterrupted experience. Studio staff will never send unsolicited direct messages asking for personal credentials, login codes, or wallet authorization.

| Practice | Recommended Behavior (Do) | Discouraged Behavior (Don't) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Direct Messaging** | Contact moderators via public support tickets | Respond to unsolicited DMs claiming to offer free game keys |
| **Feedback Sharing** | Group thoughts logically with screenshots and system specs | Spam general channels with one-line demands or complaints |
| **Interacting with Devs** | Treat team members respectfully during public AMAs | Repeatedly tag individual developers with personal questions |
| **Spoilers & Leaks** | Use spoiler tags when discussing closed-test story content | Share non-public test footage outside designated channels |
| **Community Conduct** | Welcome newcomers and share gameplay insights kindly | Engage in platform tribalism or hostile gaming debates |

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Moonfrost Discord

### Where can I find the official invite link for the Moonfrost Discord?
You should always obtain the invite link from legitimate platforms such as the official Oxalis Games website, verified Twitter/X accounts, or official Steam community boards. Avoid accepting invites sent through unsolicited private messages, as malicious actors sometimes create copycat servers to distribute phishing links.

### Are playtest keys guaranteed if I join the Moonfrost Discord?
Joining the server does not guarantee immediate access to private testing rounds. However, the Moonfrost Discord is the primary hub where alpha applications, playtest sign-up surveys, and key drops are announced. Staying active and opting into playtest notification roles gives you the best chance of securing a spot.

### How often do developers interact with players on the server?
The development team regularly monitors public channels, particularly during major feature reveals and testing periods. While studio members cannot reply to every individual message, they frequently participate in scheduled ask-me-anything (AMA) sessions, post informal updates in preview channels, and read community feedback boards.

### Can I share fan art and game suggestions directly with the team?
Yes. The server features dedicated channels specifically designed for community creativity and feature suggestions. By posting your artwork or game ideas in those designated spaces, you ensure your contributions are seen by community managers and fellow enthusiasts without cluttering general chat rooms.
