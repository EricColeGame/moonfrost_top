## Friendship Mechanics: How Gifting and Bonding Work

Social progression in cozy RPGs hinges on consistent, meaningful interactions. In Moonfrost, building bonds with **Moonfrost characters** relies on three primary pillars: daily conversation, fulfilling bulletin board requests, and presenting thoughtful gifts. 

Each character maintains an internal preference chart categorized into loved, liked, neutral, disliked, and hated items. Presenting a loved item triggers an expressive animated reaction and a substantial jump in friendship points. Conversely, offering something a villager despises can set your relationship back, causing them to grumble or refuse to speak for the rest of the day.

| Gift Category | Friendship Point Multiplier | Character Reaction Style | Common Item Examples |

To raise your standing efficiently, keep your inventory organized with universal "Liked" items such as seasonal wildflowers or basic baked goods. ## Frequently Asked Questions About Moonfrost Characters

### Can you romance multiple Moonfrost characters at the same time?
Player experience with the romance system indicates that you can raise friendship and view early heart events for multiple candidates simultaneously. However, committing to an official partnership typically requires giving a unique courtship token to a single partner, locking in an exclusive relationship path.

### What happens if you give a disliked gift to Moonfrost characters?
Presenting an item that an NPC dislikes or hates results in an immediate reduction of friendship points. While a single accidental mistake will not permanently ruin your relationship, repeated bad gifts will stall heart level progress and cause the villager to respond with irritated dialogue.

### Do Moonfrost characters follow the same schedule on rainy days?
No. Adverse weather conditions cause townsfolk to alter their usual routines. Outdoor workers such as fishermen, carpenters, and farmers will take shelter indoors, open their workshops earlier, or spend extended afternoons socializing inside the community tavern.

### How do Moonfrost characters unlock new crafting recipes for players?
As your heart levels increase with specific villagers, they will periodically send you letters containing blueprints, crafting formulas, and cooking recipes tailored to their profession. Reaching mid-to-high friendship tiers is the primary method for acquiring advanced artisan equipment and rare decor items.
